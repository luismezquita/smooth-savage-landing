Privacy Policy

Smooth Savage | Effective date: May 2026

Smooth Savage is a mobile app designed to help you discover fresh foods, superfoods, and smoothie recipes. We built it with privacy in mind. The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

No personal data collected

Smooth Savage does not collect personal data. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, or other identifying information. There is no account registration or login required to use the app.

No tracking or analytics

We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party tracking services. Your usage of the app is not monitored or recorded.

Favorites stored locally only

When you save items to Favorites, that data is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to a server or shared with anyone. If you uninstall the app, saved favorites are removed with it.

Offline-first app

Smooth Savage is designed to work offline. Foods, superfoods, smoothie recipes, and images are bundled with the app. No internet connection is required after installation for the app content.

No third-party sharing

Since we collect no data, there is nothing to share with third parties. We do not sell, rent, or transfer user information.

Children's privacy

Smooth Savage does not collect data from anyone, including children.
